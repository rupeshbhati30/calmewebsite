What’s in a bottle of Calme?+

Each bottle contains 60 mixed berry gummies. The supplied label lists 30 servings per bottle, with a serving size of two gummies.

Which ingredients are listed on pack?+

Ashwagandha extract (300 mg), passion flower extract (100 mg), L-theanine (100 mg), chamomile extract (50 mg), and vitamin B6 (1.4 mg) per two-gummy serving. Please refer to your product label for the full ingredient list.

How should I take Calme?+

The supplied label suggests adults take two gummies daily, preferably in the evening, or as recommended by a healthcare professional. Read the label and consult your physician if you have questions about use.

Is Calme a medicine or a treatment for anxiety?+

No. Calme is a dietary supplement. The package says it is not for medicinal use and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease. It is not a substitute for professional medical or mental-health care.
