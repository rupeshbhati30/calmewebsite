Privacy policy

This page explains how the Calme order form collects, stores and uses an order request.

Details collected

The order form collects your name, phone number, delivery address, city, PIN code, bottle quantity, preferred confirmation method, optional order note and your privacy-consent choice. Please do not include medical or other sensitive personal information in the order note.

Order form data is stored in Supabase

When you check the consent box and submit the order form, the website sends these details to Calmveda’s Supabase project for storage in the order_enquiries table so the team can respond and confirm your request. The request includes the form fields and server-generated submission time. The public website can insert a new request but cannot retrieve, list, edit or delete stored requests. Authorized people with access to Calmveda’s Supabase project may view the table.

WhatsApp and phone contact

After the database saves a request successfully, the page prepares a WhatsApp message addressed to +91 99967 56587. You decide whether to open WhatsApp, review the message and tap Send. If sent, information is also shared with WhatsApp and processed under its own terms and privacy policy. If you call Calmveda, information you share on the call is handled by the business team to respond and confirm an order.

Retention and requests

Calmveda uses order details to respond to enquiries, call to confirm orders, check delivery availability and communicate about fulfilment. The business should define and apply a retention period and deletion process for unneeded enquiries. For an access or deletion request, contact Calmveda using the details below.

Third-party services and technical data

The page loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive technical request information such as your IP address and browser details. WhatsApp is opened only when you choose a WhatsApp link. Links to Instagram are also provided. Those services operate under their own privacy policies.

This site has no account, analytics or advertising-cookie feature. Your browser may still store technical data independently of this website.

Children and health information

This site is intended for adults enquiring about a dietary supplement. Do not send a child’s information or private health information through the form or WhatsApp order message. Health questions should be discussed with a qualified healthcare professional.

Contact

For privacy questions about an order enquiry, contact Calmveda Health Care by calling +91 99967 56587 or using WhatsApp.

Before launch, confirm that this description matches the project access, retention practices, Supabase region and applicable privacy requirements. Never place a Supabase secret or service-role key in the website.
